People do not ask neat benchmark questions.

"Floodwater touch hue baby bottle nipples/pacifiers ko bleach se sanitize karke use kar sakte hain?"

A plausible generic answer says yes, sanitize with bleach. The official CDC exception says no: throw these items out.

What Beacon adds on top of Gemma.

The model is local. The evidence is local. The app controller owns tool access, hidden safety instructions, prompt assembly, and citations.

submission framing

Beacon targets Global Resilience and Safety & Trust. It does not claim Ollama is the whole app; Ollama runs the local model while Beacon owns offline official documents and the tool loop.

The claim is simple: crisis guidance should be local, cited, and humble when evidence is missing.

Trained with Unsloth on the Beacon tool-use behavior path.
Runs locally through Ollama with a Q4_K_M GGUF artifact.
Uses an offline official-doc tool owned by the Beacon controller.
Positions mobile deployment as the next packaging step, not as a current overclaim.
